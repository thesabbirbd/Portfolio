## 💡 Suggestions for Next-Generation Autonomous AI Agent Features
As requested, here are practical, reusable AI agent feature suggestions designed for everyday Bangladeshi businesses and global creators that can be built locally with zero paid APIs:

1. **Auto-Daraz SKU Optimizer Agent (Category: E-Commerce / Media)**:
   - *Problem*: Sellers manually copy product specs and struggle with Daraz category attributes.
   - *Feature*: An agent that accepts a product title and image, extracts technical attributes (size, material, warranty), and generates optimized Daraz bullet points with required Bangla and English tags.
2. **Cheque & Bank Slip OCR Auditor Agent (Category: Finance & POS)**:
   - *Problem*: Shopkeepers receive physical bank deposit slips or cheques and manually type routing numbers and amounts.
   - *Feature*: Client-side Tesseract/Canvas engine that detects Bangladeshi bank cheque MICR lines, validates amount in numbers vs. words, and outputs reconciliation CSVs.
3. **SMS / Email Cold Outreach Personalizer Agent (Category: Marketing)**:
   - *Problem*: Sending generic bulk SMS results in low engagement.
   - *Feature*: Reusable template injector that merges customer order history into personalized, respectful Bangla SMS reminders without spam triggers.
4. **Local PDF Resume & Portfolio Sanitizer (Category: Privacy)**:
   - *Problem*: Job applicants share CVs publicly on LinkedIn with phone numbers and private addresses exposed.
   - *Feature*: One-click privacy mask replacing personal addresses with district-level summaries (`Uttara, Dhaka`) and protecting phone numbers with contact request links.
