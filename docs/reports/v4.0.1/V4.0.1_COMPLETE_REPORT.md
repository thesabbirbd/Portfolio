### Sound — "max", but safe
- **On by default at High.** Browsers block audio until the visitor's first tap, click or key press anywhere, so nothing plays before that.
- **High = 2× the previous maximum:** −6.6 dBFS, versus −12.6 dBFS before. Low and Medium sit below it.
- A soft-clip limiter means it never clips.
- **Settings:** a speaker icon (true mute, remembered) plus **Low / Medium / High**, plus Test. The slider is gone.
- **Scroll soundscape:**
  - a soft mechanical tick while scrolling;
  - **the faster you scroll, the quieter it gets** (down to ¼ on a hard fling);
  - a clock-like "tik… tikk… tiiik" through the career timeline, rising in pitch and length;
  - a sweep when a new chapter enters.
- **Clicks:**
  - every clickable thing gets the **theme's own tap**: Orange = a small gold bell, Green = a soft wooden knock, Blue = a crisp digital blip;
  - **clicking something that isn't clickable plays a soft low "miss"**;
  - component-specific cues (copy, error, terminal, theme switch …) still play, and the global layer never doubles them.

### Mobile right-side avatar
- It is now **the same size (46 px) and in the same column as Settings**, directly above it.
- **While you scroll it slides down behind Settings** and becomes inert. **When scrolling stops (~0.9 s) it rises back up**, like the bottom nav.
- Measured: while scrolling, the avatar sits 1 px off the gear position; at rest it is 10 px above Settings.

### Luxury, golden, theme-unique, motion-video feel
- **Gold accents across every theme:**
  - a gold hairline on every card;
  - gold section numbers;
  - a gold scroll-progress thread across the top of every page;
  - a one-time gold sheen over the hero wordmark;
  - gold-rimmed hero chips.
- **Backdrop** (`AmbientBackdrop`, CSS only, every device):
  - gold deco rays, equaliser bars, a self-drawing line graph and gold dust;
  - a **drift layer of lightweight CSS-3D objects that rise slowly as you scroll down**. It uses a CSS scroll timeline (zero JS).

| Theme | Objects |
|---|---|
| Orange · Royal | gold gyroscope, spinning gold coins, gold wire cube, art-deco chevrons |
| Green · Studio | solid Bauhaus cubes, spheres, half-disc, triangle, stripes |
| Blue · Tech | **data streams down both edges, a radar sweep with blips, a network graph with travelling packets, a mini live dashboard (bars + sparkline), a wireframe cube-in-cube, a wire globe, circuit traces, a perspective grid floor** |

- **Heavy 3D removed:** the optional three.js/WebGL scene and the parallax "IT widget" chips are no longer loaded. The Settings "3D & spatial effects" switch now controls only the ambient hero video and the interactive globe.
- **Phone hero now matches the desktop:** spinning rings and floating bob on every device, plus the six orbiting skill chips (icons on phones, labels from `sm`). Each chip links to its evidence.
- **Cinematic scroll:**
  - cards tip up out of depth as they enter (3D reveal);
  - on scroll-out, the hero copy and portrait leave at different speeds, like a camera move.
  - All of this is transform/opacity on CSS scroll timelines, and reduced motion disables it.

## 6. Sound — tested how
- `sound-audit` ⟨SOUND⟩. It covers:
  - silent before a gesture;
  - the first tap unlocks;
  - the scroll soundscape is throttled;
  - the speaker mute is a true mute and persists;
  - the level control persists;
  - offline-rendered levels: Low −17.1 / Medium −11.1 / High −6.6 dBFS.
