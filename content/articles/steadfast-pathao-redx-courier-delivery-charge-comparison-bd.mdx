## The E-Commerce Logistics Landscape in Bangladesh

For F-Commerce and online retail merchants in Bangladesh, parcel shipping costs directly dictate net profit margins. With millions of doorstep deliveries happening monthly, choosing the optimal courier partner between **Steadfast Courier**, **Pathao Courier**, and **RedX Delivery** is a critical operational decision.

## Cost Breakdown: Base Tariff vs Weight & COD Charges

Every courier invoice consists of three key pricing tiers:

### 1. Base Delivery Charge
- **Inside Dhaka (0–1 KG):** Typically ৳60 to ৳70 for next-day or 48-hour delivery.
- **Sub-Dhaka / Suburbs (Savar, Gazipur, Keraniganj):** ৳90 to ৳100.
- **Outside Dhaka (Divisional & District Levels):** ৳120 to ৳130.

### 2. Weight Surcharge
For packages exceeding 1.0 KG, each additional kilogram incurs a surcharge of approximately **৳20 per KG**.

### 3. Cash on Delivery (COD) Collection Fee
When couriers collect cash payments from the customer upon delivery, they charge a collection fee:
- **Steadfast Courier:** 1.0% COD fee.
- **Pathao Courier:** 1.0% COD fee (often 0.5% during merchant promos).
- **RedX Delivery:** 1.0% COD fee.
- **eCourier BD:** 0.5% to 1.0% depending on volume tier.

## Return Charge & Merchant Risk

If a buyer refuses delivery at their doorstep, merchants are charged return fees (often 50% to 100% of the base delivery rate). Minimizing fake orders through customer phone verification and checking district postcodes before dispatching is essential.
