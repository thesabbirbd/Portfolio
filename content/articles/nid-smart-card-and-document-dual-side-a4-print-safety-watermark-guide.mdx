## The Identity Fraud Threat in Bangladesh Photocopy Shops

In Bangladesh, citizens routinely submit photocopies of their National Identity Card (NID), Smart Card, Driving License, or Student ID for everyday transactions:
1. Opening bank accounts and MFS accounts (bKash, Nagad, Rocket).
2. Biometric SIM card registration and verification.
3. Apartment tenancy agreements and police tenant verification forms.
4. Job applications and school admissions.

Unfortunately, thousands of plain, un-watermarked NID photocopies circulate unprotected. Fraudulent syndicates can easily reuse a plain photocopy to register unverified SIM cards or open dummy accounts.

The safest preventive measure recommended by cybersecurity experts and Bangladesh Police is **diagonal purpose-bound watermarking** across the card photocopy, ensuring it cannot be repurposed for any unauthorized service.

## 1. Physical Specifications: CR80 Standards vs. ISO 216 A4

An authentic smart ID card follows the global **ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 (CR80)** standard:

- **Card Physical Width**: 85.60 mm (3.370 inches)
- **Card Physical Height**: 53.98 mm (2.125 inches)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1.5858 : 1
- **A4 Sheet Dimensions**: 210 mm × 297 mm (ISO 216)

When printing both Front and Back on an A4 sheet at standard print resolution (150 to 300 DPI):

```text
CR80 Card at 150 DPI: 506 px × 319 px
CR80 Card at 300 DPI: 1012 px × 638 px
A4 Sheet at 150 DPI:  1240 px × 1754 px
A4 Sheet at 300 DPI:  2480 px × 3508 px
```

Aligning both cards side-by-side or top-to-bottom with a 60px margin creates an official, clean layout ready for single-click laser or inkjet printing.

## 2. Anatomy of an Effective Anti-Misuse Security Watermark

An effective watermark must balance two competing constraints:
- **Legibility**: Officials must clearly read the citizen's name, parent's names, date of birth, and NID number.
- **Tamper-Resistance**: An attacker should not be able to wipe the watermark using simple paint or erase tools without destroying the card details underneath.

```text
[Recommended Watermark Format]
"শুধুমাত্র ব্র্যাক ব্যাংক একাউন্ট খোলার জন্য প্রযোজ্য — অন্য কোনো কাজে ব্যবহার নিষিদ্ধ"
[তারিখ: ১৫/১১/২০২৬]
```

### Watermark Technical Parameters:
1. **Angle**: -25 degrees diagonal across the card surface.
2. **Opacity**: 25% to 35% alpha channel (`globalAlpha = 0.28`).
3. **Color**: High-visibility crimson red (`#DC2626`) or dark charcoal (`#1F2937`).
4. **Timestamping**: Including the exact date of submission ensures that old copies cannot be used years later.
