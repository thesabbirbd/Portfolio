## The Paper Khata Dilemma in Local Retail

Across Bangladesh, thousands of grocery stores, pharmacies, and wholesale businesses still rely on paper ledger notebooks (*Baki Khata*). While physical notebooks are fast for jotting down sales during busy store hours, they suffer from three critical bottlenecks:

1. **Math Errors**: Manually calculating line-item totals and daily sums often leads to discrepancies.
2. **Time Drain**: Spending 1 to 2 hours every evening retyping numbers into a spreadsheet or ledger book.
3. **No Searchability**: Finding customer purchase history from three months ago requires flipping through hundreds of paper pages.

---

## 1. Bridging the Gap: Bangla Numeral Parsing

The primary technical hurdle in digitizing local ledgers has been the mixed use of Bengali numerals (`০, ১, ২, ৩, ৪, ৫, ৬, ৭, ৮, ৯`) and English digits (`0-9`), combined with colloquial unit descriptions such as `কেজি`, `লিটার`, and `পিস`.

A modern tokenization engine solves this by:
- Normalizing all Bengali digit characters into standardized ASCII numerals.
- Parsing line items using regex anchors that isolate the item description from quantity and price.
- Calculating implicit unit prices when only total amount and quantity are provided.

```python
# Conceptual transformation pipeline
raw_text = "মিনিকেট চাল ৫ কেজি ৩৭৫ টাকা"
normalized = to_english_digits(raw_text)  # "মিনিকেট চাল 5 কেজি 375 টাকা"
item = parse_line(normalized)
# Result: {"name": "মিনিকেট চাল", "qty": 5, "unit": "কেজি", "unit_price": 75, "total": 375}
```
