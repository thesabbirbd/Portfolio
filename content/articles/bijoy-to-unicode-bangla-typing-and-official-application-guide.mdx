## The Historical Divide Between Legacy Bijoy (ANSI) and Universal Unicode

For over three decades, Bengali computing experienced a fundamental schism between two typographic architectures:
1. **Legacy ANSI Encoding (Bijoy / SutonnyMJ)**: Introduced in late 1988, Bijoy relied on remapping Latin ASCII character codes (Windows-1252 / ANSI) directly to glyph segments of the SutonnyMJ font. Because characters were not assigned genuine semantic Unicode code points, text written in Bijoy appeared as gibberish (e.g. `Avgvi †mvbvi evsjv` instead of `আমার সোনার বাংলা`) whenever the specific Sutonny font was absent on the target machine.
2. **Universal Unicode UTF-8 (Avro / Vrinda / Kalpurush)**: Standardized by the Unicode Consortium and popularized by Avro Keyboard in 2003, UTF-8 assigns immutable semantic code points (`U+0980` through `U+09FF`) to Bengali vowels, consonants, and signs. Unicode works natively across web browsers, smartphones, databases, and search engines.

Despite the universality of Unicode, government secretariats, courts, and printing presses across Bangladesh continue to require SutonnyMJ ANSI files for Adobe Illustrator and legacy Microsoft Word layouts.

## 2. Official Administrative Application Standards in Bangladesh

When drafting official letters to government ministries, deputy commissioners (DC), universities, or employers, strict protocols must be followed:

### Standard Structure of a Bengali Administrative Petition
1. **Recipient & Date**: Designation (e.g., *বরাবর, জেলা প্রশাসক / বিভাগীয় প্রধান*), Office Address, and Date.
2. **Subject Line (বিষয়)**: Clear, concise intent (e.g., *বিষয়: চারিত্রিক সনদপত্র প্রাপ্তির জন্য আবেদন*).
3. **Formal Salutation (জনাব/মহোদয়)**: Respectful acknowledgment of authority.
4. **Main Statement (বিনীত নিবেদন এই যে...)**: Exact facts, institutional identifiers, and rationale.
5. **Closing Prayer (অতএব, প্রার্থনা...)**: Explicit request for authorization.
6. **Sign-off (বিনীত নিবেদক)**: Full signature, name, phone number, and address.
