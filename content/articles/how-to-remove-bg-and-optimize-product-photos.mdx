## Why Product Photography Makes or Breaks Online Sales

In Bangladeshi e-commerce—whether selling on Daraz, Facebook Marketplace, or your own online storefront—customer trust is built within the first 500 milliseconds of landing on a product page.

A raw smartphone photo taken on a cluttered bedsheet or tiled floor communicates amateur quality. Conversely, a clean cutout on an alabaster studio pedestal with a subtle contact shadow immediately establishes commercial legitimacy.

Traditionally, achieving this required either an expensive Photoshop subscription or uploading private customer photos to paid cloud services that charge per credit. Today, modern HTML5 Canvas matting and browser WebAssembly allow you to perform professional studio background removal entirely inside your browser with 100% data privacy.

---

## 1. The 3 Elements of High-Converting Product Photos

1. **Clean Edge Matting**: Preserving delicate edges, such as fabric hems or metallic borders, without leaving jagged green or white pixel halos.
2. **Neutral Studio Lighting**: An off-white or soft cream gradient (`#FAF8F5` or `#F3F4F6`) that makes product colors pop while passing catalog compliance checks.
3. **Natural Contact Shadow**: A grounded drop shadow (`blur: 16px, opacity: 0.18`) underneath the item to prevent the product from looking like it is floating in empty space.
