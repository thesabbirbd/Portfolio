## The Purpose of a Marriage Biodata

In Bangladeshi society, a matrimonial biodata (*পাত্র-পাত্রীর বিয়ের বায়োডাটা*) serves as the initial introduction between two families. Beyond a simple resume, a well-structured biodata conveys religious values, personality, educational background, professional trajectory, and family roots with clarity and honesty.

## Essential Sections of an Authentic Biodata

### 1. Spiritual & Religious Identity
In Islamic matrimonial contexts, transparency regarding religious practice establishes clear expectations:
- Regularity of daily five prayers (*সালাত / নামাজ*).
- Observance of modesty and hijab / beard (*পর্দা ও সুন্নতি জীবনযাপন*).
- Commitment to halal earnings and ethical living.

### 2. Physical & Personal Details
- Full legal name and year of birth.
- Accurate height and physical appearance.
- Blood group (vital for future medical compatibility).
- Marital status (never married / divorced / widowed).

### 3. Educational & Career Qualifications
List major educational milestones chronologically:
- **Secondary & Higher Secondary:** SSC and HSC GPA with passing year and institution.
- **Graduation & Post-Graduation:** Major field of study, university name, and degree.
- **Current Profession:** Job designation, organization, work location, and approximate income bracket.

### 4. Family Background
- Father's and mother's occupations and ancestral roots.
- Number of brothers and sisters, their educational status, and marital status.
- Paternal and maternal uncle references (*চাচা ও মামাদের পরিচয়*).

### 5. Expectations from Future Life Partner
Clearly outlining what you value in a spouse prevents mutual disappointments. Specify expectations regarding religious devotion, mutual respect, family values, and lifestyle preferences.
