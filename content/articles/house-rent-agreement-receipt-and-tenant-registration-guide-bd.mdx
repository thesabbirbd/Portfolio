## Legal and Administrative Fundamentals of Residential Tenancy in Bangladesh

Renting a residential apartment or commercial floor in urban Bangladesh (Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet) involves critical legal and administrative formalities governed by the **Premises Rent Control Act, 1991** and security regulations mandated by the **Dhaka Metropolitan Police (DMP)**.

Failing to document rent payments or neglecting police tenant verification can lead to costly legal disputes or severe regulatory penalties for landlords and tenants alike.

---

## 1. Key Clauses in a Bangladeshi Residential Tenancy Agreement

A valid residential tenancy agreement should ideally be printed on **Non-Judicial Stamps (নন-জুডিশিয়াল স্ট্যাম্প)** of requisite value (typically ৳ 300) and signed by both the landlord (*বাড়ির মালিক*) and tenant (*ভাড়াটিয়া*), witnessed by two local referees.

### Mandatory Legal Clauses
1. **Identification of Premises**: Exact floor, unit number, holding number, road number, and postal code.
2. **Tenancy Duration & Renewal**: Standard period is typically 11 months or 1 to 2 years, with specific notice periods for termination (usually 1 or 2 months prior written notice).
3. **Monthly Rent & Utility Splitting**: Clarify whether monthly service charges (lift, generator, security guards), WASA water bills, and Titas gas bills are included in the base rent or billed separately.
4. **Advance Security Deposit (জামানত)**: Specify the exact advance amount and conditions for full refund upon peaceful handover.
5. **Permitted Use**: Strict limitation to peaceful family residential purposes with prohibition of unlawful sub-letting.

## 2. Why Dual-Copy Rent Receipts in Bangla Words are Essential

Cash rent transactions without written receipts expose both parties to mutual vulnerability. 

An authentic **Dual-Copy Rent Receipt (ভাড়ার রশিদ)** contains:
- **Landlord Copy & Tenant Copy**: Identical side-by-side vouchers for mutual bookkeeping.
- **Taka Amount in Words (কথায় টাকা)**: Explicitly spelled out in formal Bengali (e.g. *পঁচিশ হাজার টাকা মাত্র*) to prevent fraudulent decimal alteration.
- **Payment Breakdown**: Segregating house rent, service charge, water, and electricity dues.
- **Revenue Stamp (রেভিনিউ স্ট্যাম্প)**: A standard ৳ 10 revenue stamp affixed across the landlord's signature when cash rent exceeds ৳ 2,000 ensures evidentiary admissibility under the Stamp Act.

## 3. Dhaka Metropolitan Police (DMP) Citizen Information Management (CIMS)

To maintain urban security and deter criminal activities, city police divisions across Bangladesh mandate the submission of the **Tenant Verification Form (ভাড়াটিয়া তথ্য ফরম)**:
- **Required Details**: Full names, permanent and emergency addresses, National ID (NID) / Smart Card numbers, passport numbers, active mobile numbers, employer details, family member census, and domestic helper identity.
- **Photograph Requirements**: 2 passport-size photographs of the principal tenant and family members.
- Landlords must submit the physical completed form to their local Police Station (*থানা*) or upload records via the official DMP CIMS digital portal.
