## Managing Household Wealth: Faith, Savings and Inflation in Bangladesh

Balancing household financial responsibilities in Bangladesh requires a clear understanding of three pillars:
1. **The Islamic Obligation of Zakat**: Assessing liquid assets, gold, and receivables against traditional Nisab thresholds.
2. **National Savings Certificates (সঞ্চয়পত্র)**: Evaluating monthly risk-free yields after official bracket reductions and Source Tax (TDS).
3. **The 50/30/20 Household Budget Model**: Allocating income systematically between non-negotiable living expenses, lifestyle goals, and long-term security.

---

## 1. Authentic Zakat Nisab Calculation in Bangladesh

Zakat is payable at **2.5%** on net wealth held continuously for one complete lunar year (*Hawl*) above the minimum threshold known as **Nisab**.

### The Two Classical Standards of Nisab
- **Gold Standard**: **7.5 Vori (ভরি)** = 87.48 Grams of 22K Gold. (At current BAJUS gold rates of ~৳1,25,000/vori, this is approximately ৳9,37,500).
- **Silver Standard**: **52.5 Vori (ভরি)** = 612.36 Grams of Silver. (At current silver rates of ~৳2,200/vori, this is approximately ৳1,15,500).

According to majority scholarly consensus in South Asia, when a Muslim holds mixed wealth (cash savings, bank DPS, receivables, and gold), the **Silver Nisab** is adopted to provide maximum benefit to the poor and needy.

### Net Zakatable Wealth Formula
```
Net Zakatable Wealth = (Cash in Hand & Bank + Gold Market Value + Business Merchandise + Recoverable Loans) - Immediate Debts
```

```
Zakat Payable = Net Zakatable Wealth × 0.025 (2.5%)
```

## 2. Bangladesh Sanchaypatra Profit Rates & Bracket Rules

National Savings Directorates manage several popular retail savings schemes:
- **Family Savings Certificate (পরিবার সঞ্চয়পত্র)**: Exclusively for adult Bangladeshi women and seniors.
- **Three-Monthly Profit-Based Certificate (তিন মাস অন্তর মুনাফাভিত্তিক সঞ্চয়পত্র)**: Pays quarterly dividends.
- **Pensioner Savings Certificate (পেনশনার সঞ্চয়পত্র)**: For retired government and military personnel.

### Sanchaypatra Investment Tiers and Source Tax (TDS)
In recent fiscal reforms, the Government of Bangladesh introduced tiered returns based on total portfolio size:
- **Tier 1 (Up to ৳ 15,00,000)**: Maximum yield of **11.52%** per annum.
- **Tier 2 (৳ 15,00,001 to ৳ 30,00,000)**: Reduced yield of **10.50%** per annum.
- **Tier 3 (Above ৳ 30,00,000)**: Base yield of **9.50%** per annum.

Furthermore, a **5% Source Tax (TDS)** is deducted at source if the total investment is up to ৳ 5 Lakh; investments exceeding ৳ 5 Lakh are subject to a **10% TDS** deduction.

## 3. Systematic 50/30/20 Family Budgeting

To avoid debt traps amidst urban inflation in Dhaka and Chittagong, the **50/30/20 rule** provides a sustainable framework:
1. **50% Needs (প্রয়োজনীয় খরচ)**: House rent, utility bills, raw grocery items, child tuition fees, and essential medical expenses.
2. **30% Wants (জীবনযাত্রার পছন্দ)**: Dining out, festive clothing, electronics upgrades, and domestic travel.
3. **20% Savings & Debt (সঞ্চয় ও ভবিষ্যৎ নিরাপত্তা)**: Bank DPS, emergency liquidity fund (3 to 6 months of living expenses), and loan repayments.
