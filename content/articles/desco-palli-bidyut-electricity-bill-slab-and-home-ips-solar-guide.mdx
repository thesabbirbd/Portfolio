## Understanding Electricity Bills and Load Shedding in Bangladesh

Electricity consumers in Bangladesh—whether served by DESCO, DPDC, WZPDCL, NESCO, or Bangladesh Rural Electrification Board (BREB / Palli Bidyut)—often observe non-linear jumps in monthly utility statements. 

Understanding the mathematical structure of **Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission (BERC) step tariffs** and sizing an efficient **Home IPS (Instant Power Supply)** or **Rooftop Solar PV installation** can save significant household capital.

---

## 1. BERC Residential (LT-A) Tariff Step Slabs

Residential electricity billing in Bangladesh is structured into tiered progressive step slabs:

| Step Tariff Tier | Monthly Consumption (kWh / Units) | Energy Rate (BDT / Unit) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Life Line** | 0 – 50 Units | ৳ 4.63 |
| **Step 1** | 0 – 75 Units | ৳ 5.26 |
| **Step 2** | 76 – 200 Units | ৳ 7.20 |
| **Step 3** | 201 – 300 Units | ৳ 7.59 |
| **Step 4** | 301 – 400 Units | ৳ 8.02 |
| **Step 5** | 401 – 600 Units | ৳ 12.67 |
| **Step 6** | Above 600 Units | ৳ 14.61 |

### Ancillary Charges
In addition to the base energy charges calculated across the slabs:
1. **Demand Charge**: Fixed monthly charge per sanctioned kilowatt (typically ৳ 42 / kW).
2. **Meter Rent**: Standard single-phase digital or smart prepaid meter rent (typically ৳ 40 / month).
3. **Value Added Tax (VAT)**: Strictly **5%** applied to the sum of energy charges and demand charges.

## 2. How to Accurately Size a Home IPS and Battery

When power interruptions occur, an IPS converts DC energy stored in lead-acid tubular batteries into 220V AC household electricity.

### Step 1: Calculate Total Running Wattage
Sum the active operating wattage of devices required during an outage:
- Ceiling Fan (BLDC): ~35W each (Standard AC Fan: ~75W each)
- LED Tube / Bulb: ~15W to 20W each
- Wi-Fi Router + ONU: ~15W
- Laptop / PC: ~65W to 120W

```
Total Running Load = Σ (Device Wattage × Count)
```

### Step 2: Determine IPS Inverter Capacity (VA)
Inverters are rated in Volt-Amperes (VA). Assuming a typical domestic power factor (PF) of **0.80**:

```
Required Inverter VA = Total Running Watts / 0.80
```

For a 400W running load, an inverter of at least `400 / 0.80 = 500 VA` (or standard 800 VA to 1000 VA) is recommended.

### Step 3: Size Deep Cycle Tubular Battery (Ah)
To support a load for T hours using a 12V DC battery with an inverter efficiency (85%) and depth-of-discharge limit (80%):

```
Battery Capacity (Ah) = (Running Watts × Desired Backup Hours) / (12V × 0.85 × 0.80)
```

For example, supporting 300W for 4 hours requires:

```
Capacity = (300 × 4) / (12 × 0.85 × 0.80) = 1200 / 8.16 ≈ 147 Ah
```
A standard **150 Ah or 200 Ah tall tubular battery** ensures reliable service life exceeding 4 to 5 years.
