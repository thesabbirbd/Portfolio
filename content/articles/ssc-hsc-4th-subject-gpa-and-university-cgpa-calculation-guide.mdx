## Academic Grading Systems in Bangladesh: From Secondary Boards to UGC Standards

Students navigating the secondary, higher secondary, and tertiary education systems in Bangladesh encounter two distinct academic evaluation frameworks:
1. **Bangladesh Intermediate and Secondary Education Boards (BISE)**: The 5.00 grade point scale governing SSC and HSC examinations, featuring optional 4th-subject bonus algebra.
2. **University Grants Commission (UGC)**: The uniform 4.00 Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) credit-weighted system across public and private universities.

Understanding the underlying mathematics removes anxiety during exam preparations and enables tactical semester target planning.

---

## 1. Education Board SSC & HSC GPA Mathematics

In both SSC and HSC examinations, students appear for multiple mandatory subjects alongside one optional 4th subject (such as Higher Mathematics, Biology, Agriculture, or Statistics).

### The Official 4th-Subject Bonus Point Formula
Under Education Board regulations:
- The base grade threshold is **2.00 (Grade C)**.
- Any grade point achieved in the 4th subject **above 2.00** counts as bonus points added to the total of the mandatory subjects.
- A maximum of **3.00 bonus points** can be earned (i.e. achieving Grade Point 5.00 yields `5.00 - 2.00 = 3.00` bonus points).
- If a student scores Grade Point 2.00 or lower in the 4th subject, zero bonus points are added, but they do **not** fail the examination (provided all mandatory subjects are passed).

```
Bonus Points = max(0, 4th_Subject_GradePoint - 2.00)
```

```
Final GPA = min(5.00, (Sum of Mandatory Grade Points + Bonus Points) / Number of Mandatory Subjects)
```

### What Constitutes a "Golden A+"?
A colloquial term celebrated across Bangladesh, a **Golden A+** is achieved when an applicant scores **Grade Point 5.00 (80%+ marks)** across **every single mandatory subject independently**, meaning the final GPA 5.00 did not rely on 4th-subject bonus point compensation.

## 2. University UGC 4.00 Credit-Weighted CGPA Formula

At the university level, credit weighting reflects course intensity. A 3.0-credit engineering core course has triple the academic influence of a 1.0-credit lab session.

### Semester GPA Equation
```
Semester GPA = Σ (Course Credits × Grade Point) / Total Semester Credits
```

### Cumulative GPA (CGPA) Over Entire Degree
```
Cumulative CGPA = Σ (All Completed Course Credits × Earned Grade Points) / Total Degree Credits Completed
```

### The Reverse Target GPA Solver
When a student has completed `C_done` credits with current CGPA `G_curr` and aims for a graduation target `G_target` across remaining credits `C_rem`:

```
Required Average Future GPA = [G_target × (C_done + C_rem) - (G_curr × C_done)] / C_rem
```

If the required future GPA exceeds 4.00, the target is mathematically unachievable without retaking previous low-grade courses.
