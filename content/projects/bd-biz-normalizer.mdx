## Overview

Bangladeshi retail and e-commerce platforms routinely ingest customer contact data filled with inconsistent notations: mixed Bengali numerals (`০১৭১...`), varied prefixes (`+880`, `880`, `0`, or omitted), and regional district colloquialisms (`ctg`, `dhk`, `cumilla`).

**BD Biz Normalizer** provides deterministic, high-throughput parsing and sanitation of customer phone numbers, administrative districts, and currency formats with zero cloud dependencies.

## Key Capabilities

- **Strict E.164 Telecom Formatting:** Converts any valid Bangladeshi mobile string into standard `+8801XXXXXXXXX` while detecting the underlying carrier (Grameenphone, Banglalink, Robi, Airtel, Teletalk).
- **Bengali Numeral Transliteration:** Automatically converts Bengali digits (`০-৯`) to standard ASCII numerals before running regex validation.
- **Administrative District Harmonization:** Standardizes informal abbreviations and legacy British spellings into modern Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS) recognized districts and divisions.
- **Microsecond In-Memory Execution:** Written with zero heavy machine learning overhead, capable of sanitizing 50,000 customer rows per second on single-core CPU instances.
