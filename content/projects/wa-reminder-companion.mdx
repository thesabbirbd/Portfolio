## Overview

Official business messaging APIs typically charge per-template conversation fees ($0.05–$0.08 per message), which quickly becomes prohibitive for small rural pharmacies and SME retailers who need to notify 400+ customers each month regarding overdue store balances.

**WA Reminder Companion** is an automated microservice designed to act as an un-metered notification dispatcher. It runs as a resilient Linux systemd background service, listens for invoice due triggers from local POS systems, and dispatches courteous, formatted reminder messages with PDF receipts.

## Key Design Principles

- **Idempotency by Default:** Every reminder job carries a unique hash based on `(invoice_id, customer_id, due_month)`. Even if the POS sends duplicate webhook triggers, each customer receives at most one reminder per billing cycle.
- **Human Jitter & Rate Limiting:** Rather than blasting bulk messages simultaneously, the dispatcher injects random jitter delays (18 to 45 seconds between messages) to ensure polite delivery.
- **Fail-Safe Audit Logging:** Dispatched messages, delivery timestamps, and failure events are captured in an append-only SQLite database and mirrored to a private Telegram channel for instant operator visibility.
