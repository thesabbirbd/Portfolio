## Overview

In Bangladesh, millions of retail pharmacies, grocery shops, and small businesses still maintain their primary credit ledger (*baki khata*) on physical paper notebooks. Manual transcription into accounting software or spreadsheets takes hours each week and frequently introduces calculation errors.

**Khata2Sheet AI** is an open-source, local-first document processing engine that takes a mobile photo or scan of a traditional Bengali ledger page, rectifies lighting and perspective skew, extracts row-level transaction records, and exports clean, validated tabular data into CSV, Excel, or Google Sheets.

## Architecture & Processing Pipeline

1. **Perspective Rectification:** Scanned pages are rarely flat. The pipeline uses OpenCV to detect document boundaries, compute the homography matrix, and warp the image into an orthogonal 300 DPI flat plane.
2. **Adaptive Binarization:** Traditional retail notebooks use varied ink colors (blue ballpoint, red ink, pencil) on low-grade paper. Adaptive thresholding separates ink strokes from paper fiber noise.
3. **Column Segmentation:** Spatial clustering groups text tokens into Date, Customer Name, Description, Debit, Credit, and Balance columns.
4. **Bengali Numeral Normalization:** Converts Bengali digits (০-৯) to standard numeric types, computes running balances, and verifies arithmetic consistency across rows.

## Why Local-First Matters

Small business financial data is sensitive. Uploading unredacted customer debt lists to public cloud vision APIs compromises merchant privacy and incurs per-page billing costs. Running the pipeline entirely on local hardware guarantees zero data leakage and $0 ongoing software costs.
