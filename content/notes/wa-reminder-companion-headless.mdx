## What Was I Trying to Do?

Small grocery and wholesale businesses in Bangladesh lose thousands of Taka each month to forgotten customer credit (*বাকি*). While bulk SMS gateways exist, they charge high per-SMS fees (especially for Unicode Bengali text) and have low response rates. Most customers communicate actively on WhatsApp.

I designed **WA Reminder Companion** as a self-hosted notification bridge that connects to WhatsApp Web via a headless browser instance, sending gentle, personalized payment reminders and PDF balance statements automatically.

## Engineering Architecture & Rate Limiting

```
Due Ledger Database ➔ Date Threshold Filter ➔ Dynamic Bengali Message Templater ➔ Token Bucket Throttler (4-9s Jitter) ➔ Headless WhatsApp Client ➔ Delivery Confirmation
```

1. **Session Persistence Without Relogging:**
   - Saved the authenticated Chromium profile directory (`userDataDir`) to persistent disk storage, allowing the service to restart or recover without requiring the business owner to re-scan QR codes.
2. **Anti-Spam Token Bucket Throttler:**
   - WhatsApp will quickly flag and ban accounts that send rapid sequential automated messages.
   - I implemented randomized exponential delays between outbound messages (varying between 4.2s and 9.1s) and introduced realistic typing emulation (`delay` parameters during text insertion).
3. **Template Personalization:**
   - Messages dynamically interpolate customer name, exact remaining due balance, last payment date, and bKash/Nagad merchant payment numbers in natural Bengali prose.

## Key Highlights & Performance

- **Recovery Rate:** Participating retail test merchants reported an average **38% faster debt collection** within 48 hours of notification dispatch.
- **Cost Efficiency:** Sent over 1,200 personalized ledger reminders per month with **zero third-party SMS API fees**.
- **Delivery Stability:** Successfully maintained active WhatsApp Web sessions continuously for over 60 days without disconnection.

## What I Learned

Automated messaging must prioritize customer trust and platform safety above all else. Adding an opt-out mechanism ("যদি এই হিসাবটি ইতোমধ্যে পরিশোধ করে থাকেন...") and keeping dispatch rates slow and human-like prevents account bans and maintains warm merchant-customer relationships.
