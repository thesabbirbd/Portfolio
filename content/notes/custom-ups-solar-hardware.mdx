## What Was I Trying to Do?

Frequent power cuts and voltage fluctuations in Bangladesh can corrupt file systems on home Linux servers, NAS boxes, and optical network terminals (ONT). Off-the-shelf lead-acid commercial offline UPS units suffer from heavy battery degradation within 12 to 18 months, slow relay switching (10-15ms) that causes router reboots, and inefficient DC-AC-DC inversion losses.

I built a dedicated **DC-to-DC Off-Grid Solar and LiFePO4 UPS** to power my network equipment and home server continuously with zero AC inverter losses.

## The Hardware Assembly

1. **Cell Selection & Configuration:**
   - 4S configuration using Grade-A 3.2V 30Ah Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) prismatic cells, yielding a nominal 12.8V and 384Wh capacity.
2. **Battery Management System (BMS):**
   - Installed a 4S 30A Smart BMS featuring low-temperature charging cutoff and active cell balancing (30mV cell delta threshold).
3. **Dual-MOSFET Transfer Switch:**
   - Replaced mechanical electromagnetic relays with dual P-channel power MOSFETs operating as an ideal diode OR-gate.
   - Switching transition from mains DC power adapter to LiFePO4 battery occurs in **under 2.5 microseconds**, completely imperceptible to sensitive router capacitors.
4. **Step-Down Buck Regulators:**
   - Synchronous buck converters step down 12.8V to clean, noise-filtered 5V and 9V rails for microcontrollers and auxiliary fans.

## Key Highlights & Performance

- **Zero Inversion Loss:** Direct DC-to-DC powering delivers **91% overall system efficiency**, compared to ~60% on traditional lead-acid inverter UPS units.
- **Continuous Runtime:** Delivers **8.5 hours of continuous runtime** for a multi-device setup (MikroTik router + ONU + mini-PC server pulling ~28W total).
- **Longevity:** LiFePO4 chemistry provides 3,000+ full cycles (equivalent to 7 to 10 years of daily usage).

## What I Learned

Never omit cell pre-balancing before putting a series pack together. Connecting cells in parallel at 3.65V for 24 hours to top-balance them ensured the pack delivered full rated capacity without premature single-cell undervoltage cutoffs.
