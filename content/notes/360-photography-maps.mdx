## The Intersection of Technology and the Physical World

While most of my engineering work happens in terminal windows and code editors, my work with Google Maps allows me to engage with the physical world. As a Local Guide based in Rajshahi, Bangladesh, I focus on capturing and verifying real-world locations.

## 360° Spherical Photography

One of the most impactful ways to contribute to geospatial platforms is through 360° spherical photography. Standard photos capture a framed perspective, but a 360° image captures an environment. 

Uploading these to Google Street View helps digitalize local businesses and public spaces that might otherwise remain invisible on the global map. 

## Why It Matters

Accurate mapping data is a critical piece of modern infrastructure. From ride-sharing apps to local commerce, everything relies on the underlying graph of geospatial data. Contributing to this ecosystem allows me to apply creative technology (photography and spatial mapping) to create immediate public utility.
