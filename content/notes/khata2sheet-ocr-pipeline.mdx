## What Was I Trying to Do?

Traditional retail shops across Bangladesh record daily sales, customer credit (*বাকি*), and supplier cash deposits in handwritten Bengali red ledger books (*লাল খাতা*). Most commercial cloud OCR APIs fail completely on cursive Bengali script and faint ballpoint ink on recycled paper. 

My goal was to build a 100% local, offline-capable pipeline that accepts an ordinary smartphone photo of a khata page, corrects perspective distortions, detects table grids, and parses numerals into clean spreadsheet rows.

## Architecture & Pipeline

1. **Adaptive Preprocessing (OpenCV):**
   - Converted RGB input to grayscale and applied bilateral filtering to preserve ink edges while suppressing paper texture noise.
   - Evaluated Otsu thresholding versus adaptive Gaussian thresholding (`cv2.adaptiveThreshold` with block size 15).
2. **Morphological Grid Line Isolation:**
   - Isolated horizontal and vertical ledger lines using rectangular morphological kernels (`kernel_h = cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_RECT, (40, 1))`).
   - Combined horizontal and vertical masks with bitwise AND to identify exact cell intersection coordinates.
3. **Cell Extraction & Local Inference:**
   - Cropped isolated cells with 4px padding.
   - Passed numerals into an offline ONNX model fine-tuned on Bengali handwritten digits (০, ১, ২, ৩, ৪, ৫, ৬, ৭, ৮, ৯).

## Key Highlights & Performance

- **Inference Speed:** Average processing time was **380ms per full ledger page** on standard laptop hardware without requiring a discrete GPU.
- **Perspective Tolerant:** Automated 4-corner contour detection handles photos taken at angles up to 35 degrees.
- **Digit Accuracy:** Achieved **94.2% recognition accuracy** on clean ledger pages and 88.7% on heavily smudged ink.

## What I Learned

The hardest part was not digit recognition, but handling broken handwritten lines where shopkeepers drew column dividers freehand without a ruler. Dilating broken line segments before Hough transform was essential to prevent row collapse. True client-side privacy is mandatory for shopkeeper financial data.
